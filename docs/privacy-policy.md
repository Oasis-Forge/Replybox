# Privacy Policy

_Effective 21 September 2026._

This policy covers the Replybox app ("the app"). <!-- Update it in every PR that adds or changes what the app stores, shares, or requests. Remove sections that don't apply. -->

## Summary

Replybox is free, shows no ads of any kind, and has no account and no analytics. Everything it reads stays on your phone: there are no servers to send it to, and **the release build of the app does not request the internet permission at all**. A one-time "Plus" purchase unlocks extra features; it is handled entirely by Google Play. <!-- Keep this paragraph true: the release workflow checks the built app's permissions on every merge (RUN-2). -->

## Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no servers, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and its release build has no internet permission, so what it reads cannot leave your phone even in principle. Nothing you see in the app is shared with us or with anyone else.

## Purchases

"Plus" is a one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details, and we receive no information about you from it. The app asks Google Play only whether the purchase is owned, so that it works on your other devices and after a reinstall.

There is nothing for us to delete, because we never receive anything. On your phone, uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. <!-- Once the data features ship, add: or use "Delete everything" in Settings. Any backup file you saved yourself stays where you put it. -->
