# Competitor analysis: "Notisave" (Android)

_Chosen 21 September 2026. App: `com.tenqube.notisave`, version not yet recorded._

**Not studied yet.** As of 21 September 2026 nobody has installed or used Notisave, read its listing, or read its data-safety page. Every heading below is empty on purpose: this file records a competitor that was *picked*, not one that was *observed*.

Until that changes, **`/spec` must not write a "They do" for any area.** A section written before the study opens with **Learn** and reasons from the user need, exactly as `docs/PRODUCT_RULES.md` describes for the no-competitor case. A **They do** written from what Notisave is assumed to do would put a guess into the rules as observed fact, and every rule under it would inherit that.

Notisave was chosen because it runs on the same mechanism the product depends on — a notification listener, with notification history and search — at over 10 million installs, and it can be installed on an emulator with no account, so it can be studied without linking anyone's real messaging accounts (`docs/PLAN.md` section 2, `docs/research/feasibility.md` section 6). It is a *history and search* app, not a two-way inbox, so the reply, triage and waiting-list areas have no competitor to observe even after the study.
